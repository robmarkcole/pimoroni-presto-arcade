Rainbow Barrage

Controls

InputAction
L / RSelect one of four towers
U / DSelect a floor
ALaunch rainbow missile
BUse a full Spectrum Blast
−Return to menu

Demolition

  • Target low floors to collapse everything above.
  • Armoured blocks need repeated hits.
  • Each destroyed floor charges the seven-colour meter.
  • Spectrum Blast strips one floor from every tower.

Visitors

  • Sky Spark removes a floor from the tallest tower.
  • Void Wraith locks an energy pulse onto your lane.
  • Change towers before the pulse lands to dodge it.
  • Spectrum Blast also clears airborne attacks.

Storm warning

Every wave pushes back

Enemy towers grow taller and tougher while their missiles and Void Wraith pulses accelerate. Clear all four towers for a wave bonus, then keep going until your score reaches the persistent leaderboard.
