Cloud craft

  • Landing automatically launches the next jump.
  • Flashing clouds are about to split open.
  • Aim for either solid half when a gap appears.
  • Reach the rainbow-marked finish cloud.

Sky kit

  • The green box unlocks unlimited laser shots.
  • Blue pickups provide a 60-second force field.
  • Shields stop cars and yellow lasers, but not falls.
  • Destroying a flying car scores 25 points.

Climb higher

Three increasingly wild skies

Later levels open more clouds, spawn faster cars more frequently and add pulsing yellow laser barriers. Falling or taking an unshielded hit breaks the character into pieces and starts a fresh run.
