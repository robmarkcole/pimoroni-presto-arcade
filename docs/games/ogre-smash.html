Score attack

  • Long trucks are worth more than cars.
  • Traffic gets faster and denser over time.
  • Duck collisions cost 50 points.
  • The final score enters a persistent leaderboard.

Power plays

  • Smash vehicles to reveal lightning.
  • Lightning clears the current lane.
  • Destroy tanks to arm a mega bomb.
  • Mega bombs clear every car and truck.

Watch the road

Hazards fight back

Lakes and holes demand a long jump. Ducks cross sideways and leave a splatter if hit. Tanks are the biggest gamble: smash or zap one for a mega bomb, but jump it and it launches a missile before self-destructing.
