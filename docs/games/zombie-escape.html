Zombie Escape

Escape plan

  • Collect every cyan gem to unlock the exit.
  • The exit stays closed while gems remain.
  • Use walls and junctions to break pursuit paths.
  • Reaching the exit carries the score forward.

The horde

  • Level one gives new players more thinking time.
  • Later levels add zombies and shorten move intervals.
  • Contact costs a life and resets all positions.
  • Clear all three mazes to escape for good.

Every gem counts

Plan a route through the horde

The maze remains familiar, but wall colours, zombie count and pursuit speed change from level to level. The safest route is not always the shortest once the zombies begin to converge.
